About Me

My name is Sam, I’m a year 1 Digital Media and Design student at Whitecliffe collage. Before starting my bachelors degree I completed the Certificate of Fine art and Certificate of Digital Media and Design at Whitecliffe. I chose to study art and design because I’m passionate about how they can integrate into everyday society and be used as tools to improve people's lives.I will complete my degree in 2027 and hope to use my design skills to tackle the educational and social issues affecting New Zealand. This site showcases both my Art from my Fine art Certificate and work from year 1 of my bachelor in design.
